My research focuses on the development of data-driven and AI-based approaches to improving safety in construction environments.

## Research Interests

### Construction Safety and Risk Assessment
Quantitative assessment of construction-related hazards and development of risk-informed decision-making methods.

### Artificial Intelligence for Construction Safety
Application of machine learning, computer vision, and large language models to construction safety management.

### Environmental Risk Assessment
Assessment of occupational risks associated with outdoor thermal conditions and air pollutant exposure.

### Data-Driven Safety Management
Development of predictive models and practical decision-support tools for construction safety.
